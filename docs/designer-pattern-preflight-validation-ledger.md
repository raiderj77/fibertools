## Decision gates

- Continue toward an automated designer tool only after all three are true: 5 paid manual reports; 3 customers request another review; 3 customers are willing to preorder an automated tool.
- Stop the pilot after 50 qualified designer contacts with zero paid reports.
- Do not count compliments, email signups, free submissions, social likes, or page views without payment.
