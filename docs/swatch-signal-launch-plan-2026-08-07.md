# Swatch Signal newsletter launch plan

**Promise:** one useful fiber-math note most weeks

**Primary job:** bring a maker back to a relevant FiberTools calculator

**Secondary jobs:** learn which project problems repeat, distribute original citation assets, and introduce paid products without turning the email into an advertisement

## Why this newsletter can earn attention

Swatch Signal is not a blog digest. Every issue contains:

1. one real or clearly fictional swatch measurement;
2. one transparent worked calculation;
3. one project-rescue checkpoint;
4. one relevant free tool;
5. one fixed-choice product-research vote when a genuine decision is open.

The complete, indexable explanation belongs on `fibertools.app`. The email is a concise field note that links to that owned page. This avoids maintaining duplicate full articles on a newsletter-hosted domain.

## Lead magnet

The Yarn Crafters Survival Kit is the immediate signup benefit. The signup success state now exposes the download directly; delivery does not depend entirely on inbox placement or a provider welcome-message setting.

The kit has been rebuilt as a sourced, seven-page fillable and printable asset. It corrects the hook-size source notes, replaces absolute promises with planning language, adds treated-swatch and measured-consumption formulas, and includes a project-rescue record. Keep it free and useful without an account.

## Four-message welcome sequence

### Message 0 - immediately

**Subject:** Your FiberTools Survival Kit is ready

Deliver the direct `/download` link, explain that calculators remain free without an account, set the "most weeks" expectation, and provide one-click unsubscribe.

### Message 1 - day 2

**Subject:** The swatch measurement most people skip

Show untreated versus treated width and explain why post-treatment evidence is stronger. Link to the Gauge Calculator and Formula Library.

### Message 2 - day 5

**Subject:** Use your own swatch to estimate yarn

Explain area-based swatch consumption, name its limitations, and link to the Blanket or Yarn Calculator. Do not place Amazon affiliate links in the email.

### Message 3 - day 9

**Subject:** What should FiberTools connect next?

Offer fixed choices: compare two yarn scenarios; save a project passport; translate gauge to a stitch multiple; or audit repeat math. Record only the selected category, not free-text pattern or project data.

## First eight editorial issues

1. What a washed swatch changes - and what it cannot predict.
2. Matching a target width to a stitch multiple without pretending the result is exact.
3. The two yarn-label numbers a skein calculation needs.
4. Why sleeves run short even when stitch gauge matches.
5. A dye-lot decision checklist before buying the final skein.
6. Comparing two blanket yarn scenarios with the same treated swatch.
7. Turning a failed gauge attempt into a usable decision record.
8. The project passport: what to keep after the item is finished.

Each issue can produce one on-site Lab Note, one Pinterest graphic, one short vertical demonstration, and one rules-compliant community answer where promotion is invited. Reuse the calculation; do not duplicate generic AI prose across channels.

## Revenue boundaries

- Mention the $1.99 Project Planning Pack only when the issue concerns project records, swatches, materials, finishing, or reflection.
- Keep Amazon Special Links out of email. Link to an on-site, disclosed buying guide only when a product decision is genuinely relevant.
- Do not sell newsletter sponsorships until the list has at least 1,000 confirmed subscribers and consistent opens/clicks make a sponsor placement meaningful.
- Do not create a paid newsletter. The newsletter feeds the owned tools, digital products, and validated SaaS path.

## Launch checklist

- Confirm `BEEHIIV_API_KEY` and `BEEHIIV_PUBLICATION_ID` exist in production without exposing their values.
- Confirm Beehiiv's welcome message links to `/download` and the direct post-submit download also works.
- Enable double opt-in unless current verified provider configuration and list rules justify another choice.
- Send a consented owner test to a non-sensitive address and verify signup, confirmation, welcome message, download, unsubscribe, and suppression behavior.
- Verify sender identity, reply-to address, physical-address footer, authentication, and unsubscribe controls in Beehiiv.
- Record aggregate subscribed, confirmed, unsubscribed, delivered, opened, clicked, and complaint counts without copying subscriber records into project files.

## 30-day success gate

Measure:

- landing-page qualified visits;
- valid signup attempts and provider-confirmed subscribers;
- confirmation rate if double opt-in is enabled;
- welcome download clicks;
- issue clicks to a specific free tool;
- tool completion after consent;
- unsubscribes and complaints;
- fixed-choice product-research votes;
- separately verified storefront sales.

Continue weekly only if issues generate returning tool users or useful product evidence. Change the offer or cadence if it accumulates subscribers without qualified visits and tool completions.
