## Defects found and corrected

1. Raised-pile and brushed fabrics were too close to smooth fabrics when the other measured ratings overlapped. Construction now loses additional points when only one fabric has a raised or brushed surface, and the result explains nap, pressing, bulk, and texture differences.
2. Low opacity incorrectly made lining appear useful for every project. Deliberately sheer overlays, scarves, curtains, and decorative uses now describe intentional transparency instead.
3. Stretch-dependent projects could look stronger than the fabric's recovery supported. Those projects now downgrade and warn when stretch or recovery is insufficient.
4. Tests now lock the repaired corduroy, fleece, chiffon-scarf, and cotton-jersey-legging behavior.

## Gate decision

The internal 20-scenario desk gate passes after the four repairs above. No unsafe woven/knit contradiction or known-poor pair is labeled reasonable or strong in this set. This does **not** replace the recommended independent review by an experienced sewist before deployment or expansion.
