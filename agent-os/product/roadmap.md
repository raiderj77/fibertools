# FiberTools roadmap guardrails

`CLAUDE.md` controls current scope, dates, freezes, approvals, and release boundaries.

## Current operating phase

Through the publication-freeze date recorded in `CLAUDE.md`, prioritize:

1. correctness and bounded calculator behavior;
2. regression coverage;
3. privacy and analytics safety;
4. security and fail-closed commercial gates;
5. accessibility;
6. legal and factual corrections;
7. broken-link and release-readiness repairs;
8. evidence collection for existing approved experiments and offers, without reinterpreting protected records.

Do not add a public calculator, general tool, article, guide, paid service, or major feature without the owner-approved publication record required by `CLAUDE.md`.

## Later work

After the owner lifts or replaces the freeze:

- choose work from verified search, usage, and revenue-path evidence;
- shape substantial work as a saved spec;
- validate demand before expanding paid infrastructure;
- keep recurring cost near zero until measured revenue supports it;
- promote only after focused tests, review, required checks, and direct release evidence.

## Never infer

Source code, configuration, a green build, or a healthy deployment does not prove provider readiness, offer activation, fulfillment, sales, revenue, demand, or customer outcomes.
