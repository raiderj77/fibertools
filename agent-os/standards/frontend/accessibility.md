# Frontend accessibility

Preserve or improve:

- programmatic labels and instructions;
- keyboard operation without traps;
- visible focus;
- logical heading and landmark order;
- result and error announcements;
- accessible names for buttons, links, inputs, and controls;
- clear validation tied to the affected field;
- responsive reflow at narrow widths;
- sufficient target size and spacing;
- text alternatives for meaningful images;
- motion and timing behavior that does not block completion;
- plain-language limitations and recovery steps.

Do not rely on color alone. Do not hide essential instructions in placeholders. Keep calculator output available to assistive technology after updates. Verify the affected flow with focused tests and, when feasible, a real browser at mobile width.
